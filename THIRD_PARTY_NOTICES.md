## Flowing Waves Shader

The cosine wave displacement in `src/components/ui/water-reflections.tsx` is adapted from:
https://github.com/pulkitxm/claude-directory/tree/main/shaders/flowing-waves-shader

The adaptation uses a local WebGL renderer, a light pool-water palette, viewport-based playback, reduced-motion support and a static fallback.
